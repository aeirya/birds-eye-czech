THE FASTEST STRUCTURAL WIN

Location vs direction

Jsem na koleji.
I am at the dorm. · na + locative

Jdu na kolej.
I am going to the dorm. · na + accusative

Jsem na letišti. → at the airport

Jedu na letiště. → to the airport

YOUR FIRST MENTAL MODEL

Czech is not “English with scary endings.”

Those endings carry jobs that English often expresses with word order and prepositions. Read a Czech sentence by asking what form is this word in?, not only where is it?.
